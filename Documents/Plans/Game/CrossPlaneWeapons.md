# Weapons and targeting across flight planes

Tier 3 (`.agents/references/risk-tiers.md`): the change alters CRC'd PostRender state, adds a missile column to the save, transfer, and replay formats, and spans the game Frame, the engine codec, and Agent. Line numbers cite baseline `c1420821`; the prerequisite Plans rename the files, columns, and functions named below, so the post-Plan names are used throughout and each citation names the current-tree site it stands for. Where a statement here and the code disagree, the code wins; report the contradiction instead of matching one side to the other. Paths below `Frame/`, `Network/`, and `Agent/` mean `Projects/BrokenEngineSandbox/Source/<dir>/`; `Engine/` paths are repository-relative. Flight-plane series.

## Context

After `Documents/Plans/Game/FlightPlaneClimbing.md` ships sit on one of three planes (6, 50, 100 m) or climb between them with velocity Z always 0; `ShipTypes.h` offers `GetTargetFlightPlane`, and `ShipBehaviours.h` offers `FlightPlaneAtHeight` and `ClimbTowardsFlightPlane`. `Documents/Plans/Game/ShipTypeSplit.md` gives Fighters and Battleships the Spawn-phase blaster fire at the nearest enemy ship, Omnis `AcquireTarget`, `UpdateFacing`, `SpawnBlasters`, and `SpawnMissiles`, and the queries `FindNearestEnemyShip` and `BuildShipRegistryWindow`. Weapons still assume one plane. The user decided (this session, binding): Fighters and Battleships fire flat on their own plane; Omnis pitch their aim up or down at their target; a fired blaster flies in 3D; a missile flies with a 2D velocity while its Z moves toward the plane its target is on; explosion and pusher falloff stay 3D; sim rotation stays heading-only (the Omni pitch is a firing direction, never a hull pitch); no backward compatibility.

Current-tree evidence this design rests on:

- `FrameInterpolate::IsVisible` tests X and Y only, ±65 by ±45 m (`Engine/Source/Frame/FrameBase.h:165-170`); the Omni blaster and missile target ranges are 3D distances of 120 and 160 m (`PlayersCombat.cpp:24-25,80,107`); the Fighter nearest-enemy search is a 3D distance (`Spaceships.cpp:95`); registry acquisition ranks by 3D radius and 3D angle (`Engine/Source/Frame/FrameRegistry.cpp:184-190`) and is a deterministic stream (`Frame/AGENTS.md`: never hoisted, cached, or parallelized).
- Fighter steering is yaw-only (`Spaceships.cpp:283`); the Fighter fire cone is a 3D angle against the hull direction (`Spaceships.cpp:454-458`, `kfSpawnBlasterPlayerAngle = 0.1`), so a target above the ship is never in the cone; the blaster velocity is the hull direction times `kfBlastersSpeed` (`:464-465`), so it has Z = 0.
- Omni `AcquireTarget` writes a 3D lead position through `common::ComputeLeadPosition` (`PlayersCombat.cpp:124`, 3D solve at `Common/Math/MathUtils.cpp:88-119`); `UpdateFacing` stores its normalized 3D direction in `pVecWantedDirections` (`:147`); Interpolate Update feeds that column's horizontal projection to `common::RotateTowardsPercent` (`Players.cpp:482`; FlightPlaneClimbing decision 16), so the hull stays horizontal while the column keeps the 3D lead direction decision 3 pitches shots from. Omni blasters leave along the jittered hull direction (`:292-311`, jitter is a rotation about Z, `MathUtils.cpp:60-64`); left vectors come from a cross with +Z (`:293,387`).
- Blasters integrate a 3D velocity (`BlastersUpdate.cpp:164`), take their direction from it (`:168`), sweep 3D spheres (`:233-247`, `Engine/Source/Frame/Collision.cpp:210-214,539`), and trace terrain in 3D (`:219`); the transfer payload carries the full velocity (`Engine/Source/Network/NetworkSerialization.cpp` blaster serializer); the client area light is built from the 3D direction (`:139-140`). Nothing in a blaster's flight is planar.
- Missiles: powered flight zeroes velocity Z every tick (`MissilesUpdate.cpp:209`), falling flight applies gravity to Z (`:213`, `Missiles.cpp:449`); homing is yaw-only through the cross-product Z sign (`:185-186,198-199`) and a rotation about Z (`:76`); the retained target's previous-frame position comes from `ResolveRegistryHandle` (`:167,183`); the target is dropped when the handle no longer resolves (`:168-172`); the acquire radius is 45 m in 3D (`Missiles.h:32`, registry `:185`); terrain is traced in 3D (`:322`); a falling missile at or below sea level despawns silently (`:397`). `MissilesPostRender` has no `PostRead`; the engine calls one when a collection declares it (`Engine/Source/Frame/Collections/Collection.h:90`).
- Area damage is a 3D distance with linear falloff (`Engine/Source/Frame/AreaDamage.cpp:38-53`); missiles register it at explosion (`Missiles.cpp:515-521`, radius 7 m).
- The missile transfer payload is `vecPosition`, `vecDirection`, `vecVelocity`, `alignment`, `fAcceleration`, `deltaRotationDelaySeconds`, `timeSeconds`, `nextJitterSeconds`, `fDeltaRotation`, `fDeltaRotationMaximum`, `fPitch` (`NetworkSerialization.cpp:31-44`); `IsAdoptableStatusChange` tests only the blaster type index today (`SpawnTransfer.cpp:109-117`); the harness `inject_payload` `TransferMissile` defaults and fields are at `ServerSimulationHarnessRigs.cpp:448,487-492`; `query_collection` missile rows and `edit_frame` missile columns are at `AgentCommandsServerQueries.cpp:105-113` and `ServerFrameEdit.cpp:37`.

## Decisions

Each decision is recorded as made; the alternatives are not open.

1. **Flat shooters take only targets in a height band.** `ShipBehaviours.h` gains `inline constexpr float kfFlatFireHeightTolerance = 2.0f` (the blaster collision radius 0.5 plus the Fighter radius 1.5, the smallest hull, so a flat shot can reach the collision sphere of any in-band target). `FindNearestEnemyShip` gains `float fMaximumHeightDifference` after `alignment`; a candidate whose `std::abs(zCandidate - zFrom)` exceeds it is skipped before the distance compare. The Fighter and Battleship Spawn-phase fire passes `kfFlatFireHeightTolerance`; every other caller (steering, health regeneration) passes `std::numeric_limits<float>::max()`, so navigation is untouched. Their fire cone compares the hull direction with the horizontal direction to the target (`XMVectorSetZ(vecToTarget, 0.0f)` before the normalize), so the band never narrows the cone. Blaster spawn position and velocity are unchanged: the hull direction has Z = 0, so the shot is flat on the ship's own Z.
2. **The visibility test stays X and Y only.** It models the top-down visible footprint, within which all planes are stacked; the band of decision 1 is the only height gate flat shooters need, and Omnis and missiles acquire across planes by design.
3. **Omnis aim in 3D, face in 2D.** `AcquireTarget` and `UpdateFacing` are unchanged: `pVecWantedDirections` keeps the unit 3D lead direction, and the hull faces its horizontal projection through FlightPlaneClimbing decision 16, so the hull direction keeps Z = 0. `SpawnBlasters` pitches each shot to the aim: with `fHorizontal = XMVectorGetX(XMVector2Length(vecWanted))` the fire direction is `XMVector3Normalize(XMVectorSetZ(XMVectorScale(vecJitteredDirection, fHorizontal), XMVectorGetZ(vecWanted)))`, so yaw comes from the jittered hull direction as today and pitch from the lead direction; the blaster velocity is that direction times `kfOmniBlastersSpeed`. No pitch clamp: the ranges bound the pitch. Spawn position, barrel offset, and pre-move are unchanged. The aim reticle (`RenderCombatAim`, client) keeps reading the column as today.
4. **Blasters need no flight change.** Integration, direction, swept collision, terrain trace, transfer, and render are already 3D (Context); the only new inputs are the pitched Omni velocities. A downward shot that misses ends at the sea floor through the terrain trace with today's impact effects; a sea-surface impact is out of scope.
5. **Missiles climb toward their target's height through one new column.** `MissilesPostRender` gains `float* __restrict pfTargetHeights` in `SharedMembers()` (CRC'd, saved, transferred; loaded from the previous row and stored every Update, so not in `PersistentMembers()`), added through `/add-collection-member`. Semantics: the height the missile's Z moves toward. PostRender Update writes it from `XMVectorGetZ(retained.vecPreviousPosition)` whenever the retained handle resolves (`MissilesUpdate.cpp:179-183`) and leaves it unchanged otherwise, so a missile whose target dies or drops out of the registry finishes its climb to where the target was and then flies flat (hold, no new state). A target on a plane has that plane's exact height, so arrival is bit-exact; a target between planes is tracked at its actual Z.
6. **The climb runs in Interpolate Update with the ship helper.** `ShipBehaviours.h` gains `ClimbTowardsHeight(FXMVECTOR vecPosition, float fTargetHeight, float fClimbRate, float fDeltaTime)`, the body of FlightPlaneClimbing's `ClimbTowardsFlightPlane` with the height as the argument; `ClimbTowardsFlightPlane` becomes a call to it with `gpFlightPlanes->Plane(iTargetPlane).fHeightMeters`. Missile Interpolate Update, inside the `!kFalling` block after the position integration and its `XMVectorSetW`, calls `ClimbTowardsHeight(vecPosition, rPreviousPostRender.pfTargetHeights[i], kfMissileClimbRate, fDeltaTime)` with `inline constexpr float kfMissileClimbRate = 40.0f` m/s in `Missiles.h` (clears the 44 m low-to-medium gap in about a second, faster than the missile closes 100 m). The velocity Z zeroing at `:209` stays, falling flight keeps gravity, and exploding rows do not climb, so horizontal homing, lifetime, falling, and sea despawn are unchanged.
7. **Spawn height of the climb target.** `MissilesPostRender::SpawnInfo` gains `float fTargetHeight`. The Omni `SpawnMissiles` passes the Z of the aim target's current position when its aim search (today's `FindTargetSpaceshipIndex`, `PlayersCombat.cpp:70-88`, called at `:391`) finds one, else the missile's own spawn Z, so a missile fired at a target on another plane starts climbing at launch instead of waiting for the 45 m 3D registry acquisition to succeed. Transfer arrivals restore the carried value verbatim.
8. **Wire, save, and admission.** `TransferData` gains `float fTargetHeight` (declared after `fPitch`, in `Members()`); the missile transfer serializer and deserializer write and read it after `fPitch`; the missile `Transfer` hook fills it and the `kTransferMissile` arm of `SpawnTransfer` passes it. Under the bad-value rule, `IsAdoptableStatusChange` rejects a `kTransferMissile` whose `fTargetHeight` is non-finite or outside `[gpFlightPlanes->Lowest().fHeightMeters, Highest().fHeightMeters]`, and a new `MissilesPostRender::PostRead` rejects a row on the same test with `std::ios_base::failure("MissilesPostRender target height")`. Every legitimate value is a ship Z, which FlightPlaneClimbing keeps inside that range.
9. **Versions.** `MissilesPostRender::kiVersion` +1 (new column), `FrameInput::kiVersion` +1 and `engine::kiProtocolVersion` +1 (missile transfer payload). Omnis, Fighters, and Battleships change no layout; their behaviour change, including Omni `SpawnBlasters` consuming `pVecWantedDirections`'s Z, is covered because grid saves are gated by `Frame::kiVersion` (`Engine/Source/File/GridSave.cpp:17`, `Projects/BrokenEngineSandbox/Source/Save/AGENTS.md`), which the Missiles bump changes, and replays by it and by `FrameInput::kiVersion`. No backward compatibility.
10. **Harness.** `query_collection missiles` rows add `targetHeight`; `edit_frame` `MissilesFrameColumns` adds `pfTargetHeights`; `inject_payload` `TransferMissile` accepts `fTargetHeight` through `INJECTED_FIELD`, defaulting to `gpFlightPlanes->Lowest().fHeightMeters` beside the other missile defaults, and passes the `IsAdoptableStatusChange` gate like every injected payload. `commands-server.md` follows.
11. **Nothing else moves.** Area damage and pushers keep their 3D falloff; the registry's radius, ranking, and both query windows are unchanged; `kTargetFleet`/`kTargetRandom` are still set from alignment; ship hulls do not pitch; the missile model keeps its yaw-only render.

## Risk triggers and invariants

- CRC'd state changes: Omni blaster velocities, Fighter and Battleship target choice, missile position Z and the new column. The version bumps of decision 9 reject older saves, replays, and clients.
- Bit determinism (`Documents/FloatingPointDeterminism.txt`): `ClimbTowardsHeight` keeps FlightPlaneClimbing's fixed operation order and basic operations, and the ship helper calls it, so ship climbs are bit-identical to before this Plan; the pitched fire direction uses only DirectXMath basic operations and `XMVector3Normalize`; no random draw is added, removed, or moved, and the registry acquisition order and count are unchanged.
- Z invariant: every direction fed to `RotateTowardsPercent`, `XMMatrixRotationZ`, and a +Z cross keeps Z = 0 (Omni hull direction through FlightPlaneClimbing decision 16's horizontal projection, missile direction untouched); `pVecWantedDirections` is the one column that carries Z and is consumed only through that projection and decision 3. Missile velocity Z stays 0 in powered flight; blaster velocities carry Z by design.
- W invariant: `ClimbTowardsHeight` writes Z only after the existing `XMVectorSetW(..., 1.0f)`; the pitched fire direction and velocity keep W = 0 (`XMVectorSetZ` and `XMVectorScale` of a W = 0 vector).
- Previous/current frame rules: Omni aim reads the previous frame (`AcquireTarget`), missile target heights come from `retained.vecPreviousPosition`, the Fighter and Battleship Spawn-phase fire reads the current frame after every Update (ShipTypeSplit decision 8), the missile spawn aim reads the current frame as today.
- Trust: `fTargetHeight` from a transfer, replay, injected payload, or save is rejected, never clamped (decision 8); the server builds every transfer itself; the client trusts server data.
- Collection rules: `pfTargetHeights` is loaded from the previous row and stored unconditionally; arrival restores it verbatim; `Spawn` sites keep `common::ValidateVector`; `SharedCrcMembers()` ⊆ `SharedMembers()`.
- Engine code names `game::TransferData::fTargetHeight` only inside the codec, the documented exception site.
